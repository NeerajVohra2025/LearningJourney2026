LLM->Compound AI Systems->AI Agents

Compound AI Systems?
	Compound AI systems represents a significant shift in the way we approach AI development .Instead of relying on a single monolith model, these systems 
	combine multiple AI models,tools,and techniques to create more robust,flexible and efficient solutions to complex problems.
	Unlike traditional AI models that rely on single model, compound AI systems combine various models,retrievers,databases and external tools to enhance
	performance ,flexibility,and robustness.	
	
AI Agents?
	AI Agents represent a further evolution,embodding autonomous systems that can perceive their environment ,make decisions,and execute actions to achieve
	specific objective.
	They can utilize LLMs decision-making and can interact with various tools and APIs to perform tasks such as booking appointments,managing emails.
